New hire readiness

Uniti Group Inc.

Day 1 issues: from about half to zero in two weeks
The problem
New hires were starting without equipment, access, or answers, and every team was working from different information.
My part
I acted as program manager for new hire readiness, working alongside L&D, IT, HRIT, and the HR Solution Center so every first day went smoothly.
  • Kept a new hire dashboard updated daily with background checks, start date changes, and equipment, so every team saw the same picture
  • Presented the dashboard at a Thursday readiness meeting so each team could give last-minute updates before Monday starts
  • Trained hiring managers on the recruiting process so they knew their part before and after the offer, which made both pre-start and onboarding steps run more smoothly
  • Single point of contact for start date changes, coordinated with IT so nothing fell through
  • Point of contact on orientation calls for questions on benefits, I-9 setup, and equipment
  • Texted every incoming hire on the Friday before and the Monday of their start
Scale
50+ new hires per week, all coordinated through one dashboard and one weekly meeting.
