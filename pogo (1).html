Neurodivergent hiring is my specialty

During recruiting

  • Share the interview format and questions ahead of time, so candidates can prepare instead of being caught off guard.
  • Offer accommodations in every interview invite, not only when someone asks, like camera-off, extra time, or written answers.
  • Score on skills with a structured scorecard, not on eye contact or small talk.

After the hire

  • Give a written first-week plan with clear expectations and who to ask for what, and check in the way the new hire prefers.
